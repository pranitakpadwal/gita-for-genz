# AI image prompts: Krishna/Arjuna chapter-overview illustrations

No image-generation tool is available in the environment building this
repo, so these prompts are meant to be run through an external tool
(ChatGPT image generation, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc.) by
whoever has access, then the resulting files handed back to be resized
and wired into the book (`assets/illustrations/final/NN-slug.png`,
referenced in each chapter's markdown and in `public/illustrations/`
for the website).

One image per chapter, depicting Krishna and Arjuna in the specific
Gita moment that chapter's Shloka section describes, in pencil-sketch
style, replacing the flat-vector illustration approach.

## Style guide (prepend or append to every single prompt, unchanged)

```
Fine graphite pencil sketch illustration, black and white, on warm
off-white paper texture. Loose, confident linework with cross-hatch
shading, in the style of a classic illustrated literary edition, not
photorealistic, not anime, not flat vector. Portrait orientation,
book-illustration composition with generous negative space, no
text or lettering in the image.

Krishna: a calm, warm-eyed young man, dark wavy hair, a single peacock
feather tucked above his ear, simple draped garment, often shown
holding chariot reins or a flute, expression gentle and unhurried,
never rushed or dramatic.

Arjuna: a lean, strong warrior in his prime, hair tied back or under a
simple cloth wrap, no ornate armor, expression carrying real emotion
appropriate to the scene (grief, resolve, awe, fear), often shown with
a bow (Gandiva) either in hand, slack at his side, or dropped nearby.

Keep both characters' design consistent across every image: same face
shape, same hair, same Krishna peacock feather, same Arjuna build, so
they read as the same two people across all 18 chapter images.
```

## Per-chapter scene prompts

**Fight 1 — The Freeze** (Arjuna Vishada Yoga)
> Arjuna slumped down onto the seat of a war chariot, bow fallen across his lap, head bowed, shoulders collapsed in grief. Krishna stands beside the chariot holding the reins, watching him quietly, not reaching out, giving him space. Battlefield suggested only faintly in the deep background, out of focus, not the subject.

**Fight 2 — The Refresh** (Sankhya Yoga)
> Close composition: Krishna's open hand extended toward Arjuna, palm up, steady. Arjuna's hand hovers just above it, uncertain, not yet touching. Their eyes meet. No battlefield, just the two of them and empty space around the hands.

**Fight 3 — No Neutral** (Karma Yoga)
> Arjuna standing at the front of the chariot, bow half-raised, caught mid-motion between raising it and lowering it. Krishna behind him on the chariot, one hand lightly on Arjuna's shoulder, urging him forward.

**Fight 4 — The Return** (Jnana Karma Sannyasa Yoga)
> Krishna standing alone, arms slightly open, faint overlapping outlines of other subtle robed figures behind him fading into the paper texture, suggesting the same figure appearing again across time. Arjuna kneeling in front of him, looking up.

**Fight 5 — The Grip** (Karma Sannyasa Yoga)
> Arjuna's hand gripping the bow tightly, knuckles emphasized. Beside it, a single lotus leaf resting on rippled water, droplets beaded on its surface not soaking in. Krishna's figure faint in the background, watching both.

**Fight 6 — The Loud Room** (Dhyana Yoga)
> Arjuna seated cross-legged on the ground in meditation posture, eyes closed, brow faintly tense. Around his head, loosely sketched overlapping faint lines and shapes suggesting swirling restless thoughts. Krishna seated calmly facing him, same posture, at ease.

**Fight 7 — Foxhole Faith** (Jnana Vijnana Yoga)
> Arjuna kneeling with hands together, head bowed, in a posture of urgent prayer. Krishna standing over him, one hand extended down toward him, expression warm rather than judging.

**Fight 8 — What You Default To** (Akshara Brahma Yoga)
> Arjuna standing mid-motion, bow half-drawn, face caught in a split-second of doubt. Faint doubled/echoed pencil linework around his stance suggesting a flicker of hesitation. Krishna in the chariot behind him, steady, watching.

**Fight 9 — Enough, As Is** (Raja Vidya Raja Guhya Yoga)
> Close, intimate composition: Arjuna's cupped hands offering a single leaf and a few drops of water toward Krishna. Krishna receiving it with both hands, looking at Arjuna's face rather than the offering.

**Fight 10 — Glimpses** (Vibhuti Yoga)
> Krishna standing in an open field at what would be golden hour (rendered in graphite tonal contrast, lighter background), surrounded faintly by sketched suggestions of natural beauty, a river line, a distant mountain, a bird in flight. Arjuna standing beside him, looking outward at it with him.

**Fight 11 — Too Big to Look At** (Vishvarupa Darshana Yoga)
> Arjuna recoiling, one arm raised shielding his face, expression overwhelmed. Enormous, only partially sketched suggestion of a vast cosmic form looming behind/above (rendered as faint overlapping linework and radiating hatch marks, deliberately abstract, not a literal monster). Krishna's ordinary human silhouette calmly standing in front of it, between Arjuna and the vastness.

**Fight 12 — The Real Thing** (Bhakti Yoga)
> Krishna and Arjuna seated facing each other, close, at eye level, mid-conversation, hands gesturing naturally. Ordinary, warm, human-scale composition, nothing cosmic or dramatic.

**Fight 13 — The One Watching** (Kshetra Kshetrajna Vibhaga Yoga)
> Arjuna seated, and just behind/overlapping his own outline, a second fainter identical outline of himself sketched slightly offset, like a doubled exposure, suggesting the self and the observer-self. Krishna beside him pointing gently toward the fainter outline.

**Fight 14 — Which Mode You're In** (Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga)
> Three small linked panels within one composition (or a triptych feel within a single frame): Arjuna in three subtly different postures side by side, alert and upright; restless, leaning forward, tense; slumped and heavy. Krishna's figure faint and consistent in the background across all three.

**Fight 15 — Roots You Didn't Choose** (Purushottama Yoga)
> A large inverted tree, roots sketched at the top spreading upward, branches reaching down toward the ground where Arjuna stands holding an axe, not swinging it, just holding it, looking up at the roots. Krishna standing beside him.

**Fight 16 — The Three Gates** (Daivasura Sampad Vibhaga Yoga)
> Three faint sketched archways or gates in a row in the background, subtle, architectural, unlabeled. Arjuna standing at a distance from them, caught mid-step, Krishna's hand lightly at his shoulder holding him back a moment.

**Fight 17 — Who It's Actually For** (Shraddhatraya Vibhaga Yoga)
> Arjuna extending an offering (a small bowl or flower) with one hand toward an unseen recipient off-frame, while his own face is turned back toward Krishna, checking, seeking approval. Krishna watching with a knowing, gentle, slightly searching expression.

**Fight 18 — The Send Button** (Moksha Sannyasa Yoga)
> Mirror of Fight 1's composition but inverted: Arjuna standing tall in the chariot, bow raised and gripped firmly, resolved expression. Krishna beside him, reins in hand, both facing outward toward the battlefield together, same chariot as Fight 1, same setting, entirely different posture.

## How to use these

1. Paste the style guide once, then one scene prompt at a time, into your image tool of choice.
2. Portrait orientation, roughly 1200x1600px or your tool's closest equivalent, works best for how the chapter pages are laid out.
3. Send me the resulting files (in order, or named by chapter) and I'll resize, optimize, and wire each one into its matching chapter file and the website.
