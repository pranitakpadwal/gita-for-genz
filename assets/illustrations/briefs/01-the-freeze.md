# Chapter 1 illustration brief — "The Freeze"

**Placement**: Chapter-opener, full page (or near-full), before the Scene text begins.

**Subject**: A single figure at a desk late at night, phone face-down, head in hands — lit only by the phone's glow and maybe a laptop screen. Modern bedroom, lived-in (not staged): charger cables, a water bottle, string lights, textbook shoved aside.

**Mood**: Stillness, not sadness. This is the *freeze*, not a breakdown — the beat right before a hard decision, not after a loss. Read as tense-quiet, held breath, not tears.

**Optional symbolic echo** (subtle, not literal): a faint reflection or shadow on the wall/window that reads as a bow lowering, or two long shadows on either side of the desk suggesting "two sides" without being on-the-nose about chariots/armies. Keep it easy to miss on a first look — a detail for readers who already know the text, not a requirement to get the scene.

**Style**: Clean, limited palette (2–3 tones + one accent, e.g. warm phone-glow against cool room light), flat/graphic rather than painterly — should read clearly as a small thumbnail on Kindle, not just full-page. No text/lettering baked into the image (title stays as real text for accessibility + reflow).

**Avoid**: literal chariots/armies/mythological imagery — that visual language starts feeling right by later chapters once the reader has the mapping, but chapter 1 should look like *now*, not like scripture.
