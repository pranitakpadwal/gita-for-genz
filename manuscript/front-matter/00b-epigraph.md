# Epigraph

![](assets/illustrations/final/01-the-freeze.png){width=3in}

> *The Bhagavad Gita was not spoken in a palace, in comfort, at leisure.*
>
> *It was spoken on a battlefield, between two armies, in the last few minutes before a war, to one man who could not make himself move.*
>
> *It has never needed a quieter place to work. It only ever needed someone honest enough to freeze first.*
