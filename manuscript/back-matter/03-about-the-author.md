# About the Author

Rudra Prasad Kasturi has spent more than two decades building growth and AI strategy inside some of the biggest names in Indian and global digital media and tech, including Yahoo, Amazon, Tencent, Times Group, India Today, Network18, and Byju's. He currently advises companies including CRED, Nykaa, and Cars24 on AI and growth strategy, and teaches generative AI strategy as a visiting professor at Master's Union. He's also the founder of AI Vidhyarthi, a grassroots AI-literacy initiative for Tier 2 and Tier 3 India.

None of that is really what this book is about. *Krishna Texts Back* came out of a simpler place: two decades inside fast-moving, high-pressure rooms, noticing that the oldest text he knew kept describing the exact same rooms.

He lives in Delhi.
