# A Personal Note

![](assets/photos/author.jpg){width=2.5in}

I'm a farmer's son.

The Bhagavad Gita found me early in life. As a child, I heard its verses without fully understanding them. Like many things we learn when we're young, their meaning stayed quiet, waiting for the right time.

I then spent twenty-two years in the corporate world, chasing goals, building businesses, collecting titles, and searching for success. Along the way, life kept asking harder questions.

*What is my purpose?*

*Why am I here?*

*What does success mean if it leaves the heart restless?*

Those questions slowly led me back.

Back to the land.  
Back to farming.  
Back to silence.  
And back to the Bhagavad Gita.

This book wasn't written to explain Krishna. It was written to share the conversations that began when I finally slowed down enough to listen.

*Krishna Texts Back* is my attempt to connect an ancient voice with the battles we quietly fight every day.

I hope, somewhere in these pages, you find a conversation that feels like it was meant for you.
